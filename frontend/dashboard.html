Paneer Bhurji with Dal Parathas
Ingredients Needed
  • 200g Paneer (Mother Dairy vacuum pack)
  • 1 katori Cooked Dal Tadka
  • 1 cup Atta (Wheat flour)
  • 1 Finely chopped onion & tomato
  • Basic spices (turmeric, chili, salt, cumin)
Instructions
  1. Knead wheat flour using the leftover dal instead of water to make soft dough.
  2. Crumble paneer and sauté with onions, tomatoes, and spices for 5 minutes.
  3. Roll out parathas from the dal dough and cook on a tawa with ghee.
  4. Serve hot Paneer Bhurji with warm Dal Parathas!
